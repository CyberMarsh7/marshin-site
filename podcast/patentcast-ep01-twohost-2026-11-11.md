**MARA:** Welcome to The Patent Cast — the show where we read the patents so you don't have to pretend you understood them at a party.

---

### PATENT ONE — Microsoft wants your sleepy computer

**MARA:** Okay. Microsoft just filed a patent — early this month — for borrowing the computing power sitting idle in regular people's machines. Your laptop at 2 a.m., doing nothing—

**THEO:** Like me.

**MARA:** —could be quietly helping train a giant AI, and maybe earning you a little money for it.

**THEO:** So they want to Airbnb my graphics card.

**MARA:** *(laughs)* Basically, yeah. **Term of the day: "distributed compute."** That just means: instead of one giant building full of chips, you chop the job into crumbs and spread it across thousands of normal computers.

**THEO:** Here's the backstory though — Microsoft's been building toward this for years. Azure, their whole cloud. They already rent you computing power. This flips it — now *you're* the one renting *yours out.*

**MARA:** Ships or drawer?

**THEO:** ...Ships. Not soon, but it ships. There's too much money in not building another warehouse. My only question is whether anyone reads the part where they're in your machine.

**MARA:** Spoken like a man who doesn't read terms of service.

**THEO:** Nobody reads them, Mara. That's the business model.

---

### PATENT TWO — The AI that predicts if you'll get sick

**MARA:** This one actually got *granted* this week. A company called Jumptuit — patent number twelve-seven-nine-three-five-three-five — an AI that forecasts your health risks based on your environment. Where you live, what's in your air, that kind of thing.

**THEO:** So it's a fortune teller, but it files taxes.

**MARA:** *(laughs)* A *credentialed* fortune teller. And the tech is genuinely cool. But here's the catch — to work, it needs your health data, your location, your whole exposure history. All of it, uploaded, somewhere.

**THEO:** Backstory: Jumptuit's done AI patents before — search, data-correlation stuff. This is them stacking health on top. They're building a tower out of *your* information.

**MARA:** Ships or drawer?

**THEO:** Drawer. For now. Not because it doesn't work — because the second you say "we predict your health from your data," every regulator in the building wakes up. That's a legal swamp, not a launch.

**MARA:** And honestly? That's the one to watch *for yourself.* Powerful AI in exchange for your most private stuff. You should get to say no to that trade.

**THEO:** Foreshadowing. She does this.

---

### THE BIG PICTURE — the floodgates

**MARA:** Quick zoom-out. The patent office used to be real skeptical of AI patents. That's over. Filings went from about eleven hundred in 2023 to over forty-three hundred in 2025.

**THEO:** That's nearly doubling. Every. Year.

**MARA:** Google and Nvidia are already racing to patent "agentic" AI — AI that doesn't just answer you, it goes and *does* stuff for you.

**THEO:** So the robot stops giving advice and starts booking the flight. What could go wrong.

**MARA:** We'll find out. Together. On a Tuesday.
