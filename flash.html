// browser-side flasher

Plug in. Pick a tool. Flash it.

Connect your ESP32 to this computer, choose a tool below, and your browser writes it straight to the chip over USB. No app to install, no account — and nothing ever uploads. The flash happens right here, between your browser and your board.

Runs in your browser Nothing leaves your machine No account, no install
Heads up: browser flashing needs Chrome or Edge on a desktop (it uses the Web Serial API, which Firefox, Safari, and phones don't have yet). Open this page in Chrome or Edge and the Flash buttons will light up.
First time? Put your board in flash/boot mode if it needs it (hold BOOT/G0 while tapping RESET), plug it into USB, then hit Flash and pick the serial port. We can't brick what we back up — bring your firmware questions to a Marshin checkup.

How it works

01

Plug in

Connect your ESP32 to this computer with a data USB cable (not a charge-only one).

02

Pick & Flash

Choose a tool, click Flash, and pick your board's serial port when the browser asks.

03

Done

It writes and verifies in under a minute. Your board reboots into the new tool. That's it.

Why in the browser? Because it's yours. The firmware goes from this page straight to your chip — Marshin never sees your device, your data, or what you flash. Same principle as everything we build: local, private, yours.
