A purpose-built Raspberry Pi bolted on to give an underpowered machine real function. You own the part.

Service area: close by, we come to you for just the round-trip gas; farther out, we'll arrange the trip and travel is on you. Either way, we'll usually suggest adding a Pi — or a few — to build real power into your machine. You front any hardware cost directly — we never float money on your parts. Optional power watchdog: a small unit that watches the power line and triggers a clean save-and-shutdown during an outage, so a blip can't corrupt your setup. It's a watchdog, not a battery — if you need the machine to keep running through an outage, that's a separate UPS.
